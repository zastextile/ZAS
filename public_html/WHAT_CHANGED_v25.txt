4. WHY YOUR PAGES DISAGREED ABOUT SPACING
-----------------------------------------
You asked why form cells differ page to page. Here is the real answer.

.zin — the class every input uses — IS DEFINED FIVE TIMES:

    product_master.php        padding:10px 12px   bg #fbfcfe
    part_library.php          padding:10px 12px   bg #fbfcfe
    production_ops_bulk.php   padding:10px 12px   bg #ffffff
    product_costing.php       padding:10px 12px   bg #ffffff
    product_costing.php AGAIN padding:4px 7px     <- overrides itself
    includes/customer_layout  padding:11px 13px

product_costing defines it TWICE IN ONE FILE. That is exactly why its grid
is tight and its form is fat ON THE SAME SCREEN.

I ALSO GOT THIS WRONG AT FIRST and my own test caught it: I said there was
no shared stylesheet. There is — assets/css/app.css. But it only tightens
TABLE CELLS, and its own comment says it "deliberately leaves standalone
form fields alone, which should stay comfortable to tap." That was my call
in the v17 density pass, made for phones. On your desk it is wrong, and
your screenshot is the proof.


5. THE FIX, AND WHY IT CANNOT BREAK ANYTHING ELSE
-------------------------------------------------
app.css now has a FORM layer beside the grid layer. It is OPT-IN:

    NOTHING CHANGES UNTIL A PAGE WRAPS ITS FIELDS IN class="zform".

Only product_master.php and part_library.php do that in this zip. Every
other page in the app — invoices, gate passes, proformas, costing, all of
them — is byte-for-byte unaffected. I did not have to touch them, and I
did not.

Two more deliberate choices:

  · THE RULE REQUIRES .zrow. So a TABLE inside a .zform is untouched — its
    controls keep the grid sizing from the rules above, where they belong.
    No grid anywhere gets re-styled by accident.

  · SPECIFICITY .zform .zrow .zin scores 0,3,0, which beats a page's own
    bare .zin at 0,1,0 whatever the load order. So the pages keep working
    WITHOUT deleting their local .zin. I deliberately left those in place
    this round — deleting them would restyle the ops grid, the size variant
    cards and the top bar all at once, which is a bigger change than the one
    you asked for. They come out in batch 2.

THE PHONE DOES NOT GET WORSE. Below 720px the label moves above the field
and both go back to 40px minimum. The compaction is a desktop answer to a
desktop problem and must not cost you tap targets.
