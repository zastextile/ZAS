B2. WHY YOUR PAGES DISAGREED ABOUT FIELD SPACING — FIXED AT THE SOURCE
---------------------------------------------------------------------
.zin was defined FIVE times across the app, four different sizes. Worst of
all, product_costing.php defined it TWICE INSIDE ITSELF — 10px 12px, then
4px 7px further down. The second silently overrode the first. That is exactly
why its grid was tight and its form was fat on the same screen.

It is now defined ONCE, in app.css, AT THE SAME VALUES THOSE PAGES ALREADY
USED — so nothing moves on screen. This removes the duplication, it does not
restyle anything.

Deliberately kept safe: the single rule scores the same as a page's own copy,
and a page's own <style> loads after the shared file, so ANY PAGE STILL
CARRYING ITS OWN COPY WINS. Pages convert one at a time. I removed the copies
from product_costing.php and production_ops_bulk.php only. Every other page in
the app keeps its own and is untouched.


B3. THE COSTING PAGES — WHAT WAS ACTUALLY SLOW
-----------------------------------------------
Straight answer, measured from your files: THESE ARE NOT 300-QUERY PAGES.
Zero queries inside a display loop on either one. That problem was real on
Product Master and is fixed; this is a different problem.

What made them heavy is that 841 LINES OF JAVASCRIPT WERE RE-DOWNLOADED
INSIDE THE PAGE ON EVERY SINGLE LOAD, because they were inline. They are now
two files the browser caches once:

    assets/js/costing.js         432 lines
    assets/js/final_costing.js    94 lines

MOVED, NOT REWRITTEN. I checked every line of the original block: zero lines
were lost, and none were edited. The data that CANNOT be cached — your item
list, sizes, versions, the CAN_EDIT / CAN_PRINT permissions — stays rendered
in the page by PHP, as it must.

BOTH FILES DECLARE ONLY; nothing in them runs at load. They are linked BEFORE
the inline block that supplies the data and calls renderAll(), which is the
order that has to hold. A test fails if either file ever gains a top-level
call, or if the link is ever moved after the data.

Both are tagged ?v=25 so no browser can serve you a stale copy.

ON PRODUCT COSTING'S FORM: I did not compact it, and I want to be straight
about why rather than pad the list. It has exactly THREE standalone fields —
a product dropdown, the AI textarea, and the "type a size" box. There is no
