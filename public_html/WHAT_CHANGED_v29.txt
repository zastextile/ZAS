WHY THOSE SCREENS ARE EMPTY
---------------------------
Production entry is gated FOUR times. An item becomes enterable only when ALL
FOUR are true:

    1. a Proforma Invoice has production ENABLED
    2. that order has LINES on it
    3. each line RESOLVES to a product in Product Master
       (by its saved product link, or failing that by its name)
    4. that product has at least ONE ACTIVE OPERATION

Until now every one of those four failed the SAME SILENT WAY: an empty
screen, or a search box that returned nothing no matter what you typed.

And My Work's single message was worse than silence. It said:

    "No production-enabled orders yet — enable one from Proforma Invoices."

It said that WHATEVER the real reason was. So if your order was enabled
correctly and the real problem was gate 4 — the product has no operations —
you were sent to Proforma Invoices, where there was nothing wrong. That is my
message, and it was misleading.


WHAT IT SAYS NOW
----------------
Both screens ask which gate actually failed, and name it:

  GATE 1  "No order is switched on for production yet."
          -> Open Proforma Invoices

  GATE 1b "No orders are assigned to you." (a worker, not an admin)
          -> no link, because there is nothing for THEM to fix

  GATE 2  "The production order has no lines on it."
          -> Open Proforma Invoices

  GATE 3  "No order line matches a product in Product Master."
          -> AND IT LISTS THE NAMES that match nothing
          -> Open Product Master

  GATE 4  "The products on this order have no operations yet."
          -> AND IT NAMES THE PRODUCTS still waiting
          -> "An operation is what a worker books against and what sets the
              rate — with none, there is nothing to enter. Open the product,
              import its parts from the Part Library, and give each one its
              Cutting and Stitching steps."
          -> Open Product Master

I EXPECT GATE 4 IS YOURS. Your Product Master screenshot showed Patient Gown
at "0 parts", and every size variant reading "No parts yet". A product with no
operations can never appear in production entry — there is nothing to book
against and no rate to pay. The screen simply never said so.


A HALF-FINISHED ORDER ALSO WARNS
--------------------------------
If SOME items are ready and others are not, entry works — and the screen
still says, in amber rather than red:

    "Some items will not appear in the search"
    "Ready — but these will not appear until they are finished: Patient Gown."

That is the case where a worker says "my item is not in the list" and nobody
can tell why. Now the list of what is missing is on the screen.

When everything is set up, NOTHING is shown. The banner stays off a healthy
screen entirely.


WHERE IT APPEARS
----------------
  · PRODUCTION ENTRY — above the search box, BEFORE you type. Typing into a
    box that can only ever answer "no results" is not a search, it is a dead
    end. Now you know before you start.
  · MY WORK — in place of the old message.

Both are scoped the same way the search itself is scoped: an admin sees every
order, a production worker only the orders assigned to them. A worker will
never be shown the name of an order they cannot see. That scoping is copied
from production_api.php line 11, so the banner and the search can never
disagree about what this user is allowed to know.


WHAT THIS IS NOT
----------------
It does not create anything, fix anything, or change a single rule. It is four
counts and a sentence. The per-operation cap, the packing ceiling, the MIN
rule, the rates — none of it is touched, and the test checks that those
functions are all still present and that the cap is byte-for-byte intact.

WHAT I COULD NOT TEST

No MySQL here. 1057 assertions on the PHP side and 665 in the browser all
