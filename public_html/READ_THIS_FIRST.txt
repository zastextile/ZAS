THE TWO THINGS TO KNOW BEFORE YOU UPLOAD
----------------------------------------
1. PACKING LIMITS WILL DROP on live multi-part orders — from a number that
   was too big to one that is correct. An order that said 250 will say 75.
   Nothing is lost, no saved entry changes. Tell your packers first, and
   tell them the new number names the part that is holding it.

2. FOUR NEW COLUMNS AND TWO NEW TABLES appear by themselves on first load,
   all additive and nullable:
       part_library, part_library_operations           (new tables)
       production_operations.lib_part_id               (NULL = typed by hand)
       production_operations.lib_base_rate
       production_transactions.variance_reason         (NULL = normal entry)
   Every row already in your database keeps working untouched.


WHAT TO CHECK AFTER UPLOADING
-----------------------------
The full lists are in WHAT_CHANGED_v20.txt section 7 and
WHAT_CHANGED_v21.txt section 7. The four that matter most:

  · Import the same library part twice
      -> "already on this product", no duplicate rows.
  · Rename a size in Product Master, then save Product Costing
      -> ONE size, renamed. Not two.
  · Open a multi-part order at packing
      -> the limit is in SETS and says "Held by <part>" when one is behind.
  · Enter more than planned on a CUTTING row
      -> a reason box appears, Save is disabled until it is filled.
